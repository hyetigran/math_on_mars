# Current art decisions

- Marine: 30-degree downward visual target, ivory/navy armor, cyan visor and orange accents. Retain marine v004 and the approved two-handed holding pose.
- Blaster: refined Twin Rail v002, held with both hands; exactly two barrels for two equipped ammo types. Firing cadence is not established by the artwork.
- Drifter: Droplet v003, flat lime body and floppy nub.
- Spitter: Siphon v003, straight upward crest with an open tip; projectile origin at crest.
- Charger: Horn v001, amber body and golden horns/forehead plate.
- Summoner: Nursery v001 in the splitter folder, lavender with a three-egg brood sac. Summoner behavior supersedes splitting on defeat; pictured eggs do not define summon count.
- Overmind: muscular Mantle v007, broad shoulders and heavy arms, fitted crown above eyes, one floppy nub, hexagonal yellow/cyan core.
- Mobs use a single frontal orientation for gameplay. Flat colors and bold outlines; avoid glare and shadow bands.
- Title: Space Mission, geometric ivory/cyan/gold lettering with orbital arc. Alphabet and type-scale sheets are supporting visual references, not installable fonts. Type-scale labels are proposed sizes, not measured raster sizes.
- Splash: retained 4K `02_screens/splash/splash.png`.
- Arena: retained 4K `05_environment/terrain/mars_arena_v005_light_center_source.png`. Natural crater perimeter, quiet open center and muted Mars palette.

Superseded prototype images and their sidecars were removed at the user's request. Selected assets and supporting sheets are listed in README.md. Gameplay documents, animation and runtime integration remain separate work.
