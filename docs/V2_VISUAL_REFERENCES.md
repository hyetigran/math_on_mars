## Application to the prototype

Replace the three isolated objects/crossroads composition with a connected neighborhood: attached residential frontages, a shaded lane, small fountain square, planted courtyard and growing beds at the outer edge. Keep the House, Greenhouse and Bakery as selectable prototype buildings; added houses/café frontages are scenery for judging spatial density, not additional production systems. Vary roof colors/heights and wall finishes using a coherent modular kit. Add timber details, window boxes, seating and canopies to make ground-level scale legible. Preserve Mars through the dome framework and distant red terrain, with greenery inside the habitat.

## Implications for later production assets

- Authored plots should establish street edges, corners and courtyard access. Any ordinary building type must still fit ordinary plots; these references do not authorize zoning restrictions.
- Building kits need front, end, corner and rear treatments; rooftops alone cannot carry street-level views.
- Neighborhood density must coexist with building selection and the walking camera. Roof/foreground fading and narrow-lane camera tests remain future work.
- Upgrade quality can come from better materials, articulated façades, balconies and gardens, not only more height.
- Agricultural transition remains inside the habitat. Rivers, churches, cars, exact roof styles, and metropolitan scale in the references are not new gameplay requirements.

The current implementation remains a geometric spatial study, not photorealistic reproduction or finished art. Review both views before approving final kit proportions.
