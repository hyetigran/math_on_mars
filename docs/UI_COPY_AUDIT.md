# Interface copy audit

User request: remove filler throughout the interface, including the entire Menu introduction.

| Surface | Removed or replaced |
| --- | --- |
| Menu | “Take a breather”, “Your little world.”, both browser/account-save paragraphs. The dialog now contains **Menu**, parent access and **Home**. |
| Landing hero | “A new home. A whole world to grow.”, “Big dreams. Little town. All yours.”, and “Make yourself at home on Mars…” replaced with a factual game description. |
| Landing CTA | “Jump right in.” removed; **No sign-in required** retained. |
| Illustration | “A little world of possibility” and “MARS · YOUR NEXT CHAPTER” removed. |
| Landing feature section | Removed the entire repetitive section: “Make it yours”, “From the first brick”, “Start small…”, “Watch it grow”, “A town with a rhythm”, “Plant crops…”, “Find your spark”, “Small lessons. Big steps”, and “Practice math to help…” |
| Landing footer | Removed the repeated product name and “A little curiosity goes a long way.” |
| Sign-in | “For grown-ups”, “Welcome, parents.” and the introductory save/practice/account paragraph replaced with **Parent sign in**. Password length guidance shortened. |
| Parents | “The grown-up corner” and “A little support. A world of discovery.” replaced with **Parents**. “Keep this town” becomes **Link guest town**; linking consequences remain explicit. Empty-state and signed-out explanations shortened. |
| Loading and errors | “Opening your world”, “A little off the map”, “Let’s reconnect” replaced with **Loading**, **Page not found**, **Connection error**. Network errors shortened. |
| Practice | Removed repetitive explanations of separate learning paths, bank cycling, and unlimited credit. Results now show first-attempt score and earned boost. Removed “All corrections finished”, “awarded once”, “New questions from the topic deck”, and “Your full credit is still available.” Actual question hints, rewards, repeat indicators and attribution remain. |
| Battle | Setup commentary shortened to **Applies to new missions**. “Try this one again” becomes **Corrections**. Result screens now say **Victory** or **Defeat**, replacing “Mars is secure”, “The slimes broke through”, “The Overmind is down and the outpost reactor is stable”, and “Your learning record is safe. Refit and launch again.” Redundant result eyebrows are removed. |
| Standalone prototype | Removed “A little town. A new world.”, “YOUR FIRST NEIGHBORHOOD” and the decorative exploration header. “First Light” becomes **Town prototype**. Replaced “A warm home overlooking the plaza”, “Fresh greens beneath Martian skies”, and “Bread, a striped awning, and a place to linger” with building functions. |
| Browser titles and descriptions | Removed “A little world of possibility” and “Build a little home…” across all four route entries. |

The audit covered application UI strings and HTML entries, including battle and prototype screens. Question-bank content, source/license notices, action costs, cancellation losses, input requirements and actionable errors are not filler. Historical planning documents retain their historical wording.
